\section*{Overview}
This appendix documents the queries used for the updated search in Web of Science Core Collection, IEEE Xplore, and PubMed. The search strategies combine EEG-related, methodological, and disorder-related terms with a title-focused branch for general EEG representation-learning studies.

\section*{Notation and scope}
AND, OR, and NOT are Boolean operators. Quotation marks delimit phrases; an asterisk is retained wherever it was used as a truncation symbol. Database field labels are retained verbatim. In the Web of Science query, TS and TI denote Topic and Title searches. In PubMed, [tiab] denotes Title/Abstract (including indexed author keywords), [ti] Title, [pt] Publication Type, [sb] Subset, and [dp] Publication Date.

The review limits included studies to English-language, peer-reviewed publications in its stated period. Database-specific filters are reported separately below. No English-language clause is present in the PubMed query; the review's English-language eligibility criterion is distinct from a query-level language filter.

\textbf{Supplementary identification.} We checked an EEG bibliography compiled chiefly from NeurIPS and ICLR proceedings on October 29, 2026. Of 12 candidates, MCAN (ALL-0815) was already in Web of Science. The other 11 (seven NeurIPS papers, two ICLR papers, and two journal articles) proceeded to full-text assessment and entered the primary SSL analysis. Data S2 lists their identification sources.

\section*{Part A. Web of Science Core Collection}
\textbf{Date searched:} October 22, 2026.

\textbf{Search fields:} TS in the disorder-related branch; TI in the general-model branch.

\textbf{Separately recorded limits:} publication years 2020--2025; document types Article or Proceedings Paper; citation indexes SCIE, SSCI, and CPCI-S; language English.

\textbf{Query}
\begin{Verbatim}[fontsize=\fontsize{8.5}{10}\selectfont,breaklines=true,breakanywhere=false,breaksymbolleft={},breaksymbolright={},frame=single,framesep=6pt]
(
  TS=(
    (EEG OR electroencephalogra*)
    AND
    (
      "self-supervised" OR "self supervised" OR "self-supervision" OR "self supervision"
      OR "self-contrastive" OR "contrastive learning" OR "representation learning"
      OR pretrain* OR "masked modeling" OR "masked autoencoder*" OR unsupervis*
      OR autoencoder*
    )
    AND
    (
        epilep* OR seizure* OR depress* OR "major depressive disorder*" OR anxi*
        OR bipolar* OR Alzheimer* OR dementia OR Parkinson* OR ADHD OR autism OR ASD
        OR schizophren* OR encephalopath* OR "mild cognitive impairment" OR stroke*
        OR "traumatic brain injury" OR "sleep disorder*" OR "neurological disorder*"
        OR "psychiatric disorder*"
    )
  )
)
OR
(
  TI=(
    (EEG OR electroencephalogra*)
    AND
    (
      "self-supervised" OR "self-supervision" OR pretrain* OR "pre-training"
      OR "foundation model*" OR "brain model*" OR "generic representation*"
      OR "autoregressive modeling"
    )
  )
)
\end{Verbatim}

\section*{Part B. IEEE Xplore}
\textbf{Date searched and exported:} October 22, 2026.

\textbf{Search procedure:} Three independent queries were executed in IEEE Xplore Command Search. Searches 1 and 2 used the \texttt{All Metadata} field for EEG, methodological and disorder-related terms; Search 3 used the \texttt{Document Title} field for EEG and general-model terms. The three result sets were combined and deduplicated within IEEE Xplore before cross-database deduplication.

\textbf{Interface limits shown in the search history:} Year 2020--2025; Content Type: Conferences and Journals. No language filter or textual \texttt{NOT} exclusion is shown in the recorded IEEE search history. English-language eligibility was assessed at screening.

\section*{Part C. PubMed}
\textbf{Date searched:} October 22, 2026.

\textbf{Search fields:} The disorder branch uses \texttt{[tiab]} and includes \texttt{electroencephalogra*[tiab]}. The model-title branch uses only \texttt{EEG[ti]} as its EEG term.

\textbf{Limits encoded in the query:} Journal Article or Conference Proceedings publication types; exclusion of Review, the systematic subset, Meta-Analysis, and Preprint; publication dates from 1 January 2020 through 31 December 2025. No language field is present in the query string.
\textbf{Query}
\begin{Verbatim}[fontsize=\fontsize{8.5}{10}\selectfont,breaklines=true,breakanywhere=false,breaksymbolleft={},breaksymbolright={},frame=single,framesep=6pt]
(
  (
    (
      EEG[tiab] OR electroencephalogra*[tiab]
    )
    AND
    (
      "self-supervised"[tiab] OR "self supervised"[tiab] OR "self-supervision"[tiab]
      OR "self supervision"[tiab] OR "self-contrastive"[tiab]
      OR "contrastive learning"[tiab] OR "representation learning"[tiab]
      OR pretrain*[tiab] OR "masked modeling"[tiab] OR "masked autoencoder"[tiab]
      OR unsupervis*[tiab] OR autoencoder*[tiab]
    )
    AND
    (
        epilep*[tiab] OR seizure*[tiab] OR depress*[tiab]
        OR "major depressive disorder"[tiab] OR anxi*[tiab] OR bipolar*[tiab]
        OR Alzheimer*[tiab] OR dementia[tiab] OR Parkinson*[tiab] OR ADHD[tiab]
        OR autism[tiab] OR ASD[tiab] OR schizophren*[tiab] OR encephalopath*[tiab]
        OR "mild cognitive impairment"[tiab] OR stroke*[tiab]
        OR "traumatic brain injury"[tiab] OR "sleep disorder*"[tiab]
        OR "neurological disorder*"[tiab] OR "psychiatric disorder*"[tiab]
    )
  )
  OR
  (
    EEG[ti]
    AND
    (
      "self-supervised"[ti] OR "self-supervision"[ti] OR pretrain*[ti]
      OR "pre-training"[ti] OR "foundation model"[ti] OR "brain model"[ti]
      OR "generic representation"[ti] OR "autoregressive modeling"[ti]
    )
  )
)
AND
(
  "Journal Article"[pt] OR "Conference Proceedings"[pt]
)
NOT
(
  Review[pt] OR systematic[sb] OR "Meta-Analysis"[pt] OR Preprint[pt]
)
AND
2020/01/01:2025/12/31[dp]
\end{Verbatim}
